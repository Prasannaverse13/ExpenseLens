Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Monthly subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are billed monthly in advance through Paddle. If you change your mind within 14 days of the charge, email hello@expenselens.app and we'll refund the full amount, no questions asked.

After 14 days, you can still cancel at any time — the subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period, and you won't be charged again. We don't offer pro-rated refunds for partial months.

How to request a refund

  1. Email hello@expenselens.app from the same address you used to sign in to ExpenseLens.
  2. Include the date of the charge and the email you used at checkout (Paddle sends a receipt with the payment ID — paste that in).
  3. We respond within 2 business days. The refund usually lands in your account in 5-10 business days depending on your bank.

How Paddle handles the refund

Paddle (our payment processor) issues the refund to the original payment method. The refund appears in the same currency you paid in, and any FX conversion is handled by your bank, not us. Paddle's processing fees are non-refundable.

EU / UK / Australia customers

Under consumer law in your country, you may have additional rights to a 14-day "cooling off" period. We honour that for all customers regardless of country.

Disputes

If something's gone wrong (double-charge, chargeback warning from your bank, anything else), email us first — we can usually resolve it in one message. Filing a chargeback through your bank without contacting us slows things down and may result in your account being suspended pending investigation.

Last updated: August 2026

1. The service

ExpenseLens is an Android application that helps small business owners capture, categorise, and export their expenses. The Premium tier adds AI-assisted bill extraction. Subscriptions are billed monthly via Paddle, our payment processor.

2. Your data

Your bills, receipts, and expense records are stored only in your own Google Drive — we have no server-side copy. If you disconnect your Google account or delete the app, your data remains in your Drive but the local cache on the device is wiped. We do not access, sell, or analyse your financial data.

3. Smart Extraction (AI)

When you enable Smart Extraction, the captured bill image (downscaled to at most 1536px on the long edge, JPEG format) is sent to OpenAI's vision model to recover details the on-device parser can't — merchant phone, FSSAI number, visit time, item count. The full image is sent, not a redacted snippet. Free tier is capped at 10 such calls per month; the Premium tier is unlimited. You can disable Smart Extraction at any time in Settings.
